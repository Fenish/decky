---
name: print-support-free
type: constraint
---

Every part must print without support. Slicer is Bambu Studio; the workflow is
iterative test prints rather than one final print.

Consequences already baked into the design: the Grid's pockets open downward
(switch pushed in from below) and the Key Carriers are printed flipped.

**Why it matters:** a geometry change that adds an overhang is a design bug, not
a slicer problem.
