---
name: monitor-mount-scheme
type: constraint
---

The deck hangs under the monitor: a magnet plate on the case bottom, a printed
clamp on the monitor's bottom rail, GoPro-style joints for angle. Target
position is about 20 mm below the monitor, tilted toward the user.

Measured constraints: the monitor's bottom rail is ~20-23 mm thick (clamp jaw
covers 0-41 mm), and there is only 130 mm of usable width under the monitor
before the cables start. The clamp screw is printed, TR12x3 trapezoidal; the M5
GoPro pivots are metal. The clamp ears drop into tapered sockets from above and
wedge — the taper is 7 deg because anything steeper closes the socket.

**Why it matters:** the 130 mm usable width and the 7 deg taper are both
already-learned limits; re-deriving them costs a print.
